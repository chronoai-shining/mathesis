# Private research obligations — engineering validation

This is downstream Mathesis P2-A implementation validation. It is not a live
mathematical review, an external author's consent receipt, or an upstream
coverage certificate.

## Behavior implemented

A current source-audited unresolved hypothesis/bridge becomes an immutable
private candidate. The submitting author binds a complete goal, 1–5 attempts,
expiry within 30 days and explicit research processing authorization to its exact
digest. The worker reuses the ordinary formalizer/elaborator and independently
inspects faithfulness. Only a faithful target can receive the submitting
author's cookie-session digest confirmation.

Private proof attempts reuse source/module bounds and the credential-free
verifier. Mongo/GridFS stores immutable source identities and append-only events.
A revoked, expired, changed, withdrawn or subsequently settled source stops new
work. Live CMA authorization, worker leases and referee revisions fence writes.
The verifier's target, solution, toolchain and axiom identities are checked
before any receipt is appended. The author can request a fresh audit of the
checked result; frozen trigger ids keep previously admitted prompts unchanged.
There is no automatic coverage/publication change or public contribution credit.

The same actions and readback have REST/OpenAPI, author UI and CLI/MCP surfaces.
Target confirmation remains browser-session-only. Private JSON review dossiers
(schema 3) and Markdown exports include research provenance and result identities.
No Trureturing/Pages code, contracts, publications, CI or runner policy changed.

## Live acceptance still required

A real managed CMA workspace with a usable current sandbox was not available in
this session. Previously inspected sandboxes were retired. No personal-model
fallback was substituted, and no external author's goal/target authorization was
invented. A real research obligation still needs its authorized target translation,
independent inspection, proof attempt and fresh correspondence audit. This gap
does not invalidate the engineering checks, and the engineering checks do not
satisfy that live mathematical acceptance gate.
